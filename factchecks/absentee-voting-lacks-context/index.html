Misleading False

Claim about absentee voting lacks context

A post about absentee ballots in a South Asian neighborhood reused an older statistic without the years, geography, or eligibility rules that make the number meaningful.

South Asians Jul 16, 2026

Claim

A graphic claims absentee voting in a South Asian neighborhood proves widespread fraud, citing a single county figure.

Original Post

Fact

The number is real but outdated and incomplete. It omits year, eligibility rules, and comparison to prior cycles that make the figure ordinary.

See Debunk

Analysis

Context-stripped stats travel fast. Check the year, geography, and denominator before treating a viral chart as proof of fraud.

The graphic paired an outdated county figure with a statewide headline, inventing a pattern that official releases do not support.

Prefer primary election board documents over anonymous graphics when evaluating claims about ballots or turnout.
