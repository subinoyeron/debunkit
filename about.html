About Us

Rapid-response fact-checking for communities targeted by harmful narratives.

Who we are

DebunkIt is a rapid-response initiative that counters misinformation, disinformation, conspiracy theories, and other harmful narratives targeting vulnerable communities across the United States.

We are social-first by design. Misinformation does its damage on social media—in posts, reels, replies, reposts, and clips stripped of context. That is where we work.

Who we protect

Our work prioritizes communities most often targeted online: migrants, Muslims, refugees, and South Asians.
