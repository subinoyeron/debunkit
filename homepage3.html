FAQs

Common questions

What is DebunkIt?

DebunkIt is a rapid-response fact-checking initiative that counters misinformation, disinformation, conspiracy theories, and other harmful narratives targeting vulnerable communities — especially where those claims spread on social platforms.

How do I submit a claim?

Send suspicious posts, videos, images, or messages to our tip line. Include links or screenshots when you can. We review tips based on reach, harm, and urgency.

Which platforms do you monitor?

We monitor major social platforms including Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), YouTube, and related channels where harmful narratives travel quickly.

Who do you work with?

We work alongside journalists, newsrooms, researchers, and community organizations to strengthen accurate coverage and help people recognize coordinated campaigns.

How do you rate claims?

Every published correction is sourced and verified. We prioritize evidence, context, and shareable formats so accurate information can travel as fast as the false claim.

How can I stay updated?

Sign up for the DebunkIt newsletter for the latest research, narrative tracking, and disinformation analysis — or follow our published factchecks and reports.

Seen something false?

Send it to us and we’ll DebunkIt. Help stop misinformation before it spreads — tips, videos, images, or messages welcome.
