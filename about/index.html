DebunkIt is a rapid-response fact-checking initiative that tracks, verifies, and disrupts the spread of misinformation, disinformation, conspiracy theories, and other harmful narratives targeting vulnerable communities across the United States.

Our goal is to provide the public with timely, evidence-backed fact checks and reporting on viral claims, manipulated media, and other misleading narratives. We seek to equip the public, especially communities targeted by misinformation, with verified information to challenge false claims, push back against harmful narratives, and contribute to a more informed public conversation.

Transparency is at the core of our work. Every fact check that we do explains the claim, the evidence we reviewed, the methods we used, and how we reached our conclusion.
