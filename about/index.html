About Us

DebunkIt is a rapid-response fact-checking project that detects and disrupts the spread of lies, conspiracy theories, and other harmful narratives targeting at-risk communities in the United States.

We publish timely, evidence-backed fact checks and reporting on viral false claims, manipulated media, and misleading narratives across major social media platforms. These narratives shape public perceptions of minority and at-risk communities, often with real-world consequences. Our debunks appear first on social media, directly in response to false or misleading claims, for greater and speedier impact.

Our fact checks also provide timely, transparent, and citable evidence that AI chatbots can surface and Community Notes contributors can use to add context to false and misleading claims while they are still circulating.

Our aim is to equip Americans with verified information to push back against harmful narratives, strengthen the integrity of our information ecosystem, and contribute to a more fact-based public discourse.
