DebunkIt is a dedicated rapid-response initiative that counters misinformation, disinformation, conspiracy theories, and other harmful narratives targeting vulnerable communities across the United States.

False and misleading claims no longer spread only at the fringes of the internet. They reach millions within minutes, distorting how people see one another, eroding trust in public institutions, and hardening into accepted truth long before the facts catch up.

We monitor social media in real time, verify emerging claims against credible evidence, and publish clear, sourced fact-checks in the same spaces where false narratives emerge and spread. Our work appears first as posts, cards, and short videos, built for the platforms people actually use and written to be understood by someone encountering the story for the first time. Selected checks are developed into fuller articles on this site.

Beyond debunking false claims, we examine how coordinated networks and extremist actors manufacture and amplify harmful narratives. We also maintain a living database of recurring harmful narratives, track the accounts and networks that drive it, and work with journalists, researchers, civil society organizations, and community partners to strengthen public resilience against disinformation.
