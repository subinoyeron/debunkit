What We Do

We counter misinformation, disinformation, conspiracy theories, and other harmful narratives where they spread—quickly, transparently, and with the evidence to back it up. Our work runs across the full lifecycle of a false claim: catching it early, debunking it, tracking it over time, and helping others recognize it.

Monitor in real time

We monitor major social platforms to rapidly identify emerging harmful narratives, prioritizing checks by reach, velocity, and harm.

Debunk with evidence

Every correction we publish is sourced, verified, with concise, visual, shareable debunks tailored for each platform's audience effectively.

Map the amplifiers

We identify and map accounts, pages, groups, and networks spreading falsehoods to reveal how harmful narratives are manufactured and amplified.

Track recurring narratives

Disinformation rarely arrives once; narratives repeatedly resurface, rebranded and recycled across platforms. We maintain a searchable living database.

Engage journalists and newsrooms

We work with journalists to strengthen coverage of coordinated harmful campaigns, providing verified material, expert context, and responsible guidance.

Build community capacity

We equip targeted communities through our tip line with toolkits and training to recognize, report, and counter disinformation.
